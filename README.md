# S&P 500 Companies EDA

This project is an exploratory data analysis of S&P 500 companies using Python.

The goal is to understand the companies in the dataset and look at things like sectors, market capitalization, revenue growth, EBITDA, employees, and company locations.

## What I did

* Cleaned and prepared the dataset
* Checked missing values and duplicates
* Analyzed companies by sector
* Looked at the largest companies by market cap
* Analyzed market capitalization concentration
* Compared revenue growth between sectors
* Looked at EBITDA across sectors
* Analyzed the number of employees
* Checked company locations
* Created correlation analysis between the main numerical variables
* Created different charts to visualize the results

## Tools Used

* Python
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn

## Main Findings

Technology is the largest sector in the dataset and also has the highest total market capitalization.

The market cap is quite concentrated, with a relatively small number of very large companies having a major influence on the overall S&P 500.

Revenue growth differs between sectors, and larger companies are not necessarily the companies with the highest growth.

The dataset also shows differences between sectors in terms of EBITDA and number of employees.

## Conclusion

Overall, this project helped me explore the structure and financial characteristics of S&P 500 companies while practicing data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, visualization, and interpretation using Python.

## Dataset

The analysis is based on an S&P 500 companies dataset containing company information and financial metrics.
